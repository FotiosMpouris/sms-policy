Privacy Policy

Fotios Mpouris · Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fotios Mpouris ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Fotios Mpouris Message Updates SMS program.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, when you text START to opt in
  • The content of messages you send to our number
  • Message delivery records (dates, times, and delivery status)
  • Your name or email address, only if you choose to share them with us

How we use your information

  • To send you the reminders, alerts, and project updates you signed up for
  • To respond to your messages and HELP requests
  • To honor opt-out (STOP) requests
  • To keep records of consent as required by law

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS consent and phone numbers collected for the Fotios Mpouris Message Updates program will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. We share information only with service providers needed to deliver messages (such as our SMS carrier provider, Twilio), and only for that purpose, or when required by law.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed to operate the program and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it from unauthorized access.

Your choices

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. To request that we delete your information, email fotiosmpouris@gmail.com.

Children

This program is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
